## The constraint that decides the book

The track is called **"Options Alpha Agents"** and the rules require the strategy
to include options. Our best-measured structure is *shares*. Those two facts have
to be reconciled honestly, and there is a clean way to do it:

> **The options sleeve is a DEFINED-RISK EXPRESSION OF THE BETA VIEW, not a
> separate alpha claim.**

On SPY over 884 blocks the call debit spread returns median +2.37% / mean +3.34%
/ t +2.09, and the long ATM call +1.49% / +4.21% / t +2.47. Both are positive —
and the honest reading, given `structure_lab --demean` showed every long-premium
structure goes negative once the drift is stripped, is that **this is the equity
risk premium wearing an option, not an option edge.** That is exactly what we
want it to be: it satisfies the rules, it is defined-risk, and we can say in one
sentence what it is betting on and why.

What we must NOT do is present it as alpha. Judges asked "why ARM over five
correlated names" last time; the answer here is "this is beta with a floor, and
here is the 26-year table that says our cleverer structure was worse."
