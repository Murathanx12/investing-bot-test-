## Three defects found by running the mandates, not by reading them

1. **A brand-new account cannot trade on day one.** Alpaca reports
   `last_equity=0` until the first close; the −3% daily latch read that as
   CANNOT DETERMINE and refused every entry (12/12 on staging). Every fleet
   account and the judged account are new on kickoff day. Fixed in
   `alpha/daybreak.py`: first session derives the drawdown against the
   **genesis** starting equity; no genesis → still refused, and the message
   names the missing step. **So `scripts.genesis --freeze` is mandatory per
   role before the first order** — `alpha/genesis.py` now accepts fleet roles
   (`--force` records a scratch account's true order count instead of refusing).
2. **A prior sized like an edge is a concentration bet.** Under `maximum` the
   basket put 25–34% of equity in each of the first three names. `basket`
   profile: 4.8% per name measured, 15+ names.
3. **Shares were dying on empty chains.** FLNC/NTLA have no contracts at the
   contest expiry; the direction forecast raised before the share structure was
   built. Now proceeds on stock quotes (`no chain at …; direction forecast
   proceeds`).

Dry pass under the `thesis` mandate (staging, pre-market quotes): 10 of 12
names produced a `long_shares` order at 4.8% each. The `convexity` dry pass
was cut short — the staging account went `ACCOUNT_CLOSED_PENDING` mid-run
(Murat replacing the accounts) and its key returns 401; the Railway staging
deployment was taken down so it does not cycle against a dead key.

## The number on the thesis account

The theme universe is **down 20–50% over the last 20 sessions** (OUST −50%,
AEVA −51%, IONQ −40%, BE −46%, FCEL −50%; median ≈ −30%) at 60–170%
annualised vol. Five sessions at 100% vol is a ~14% one-sigma swing. That is
the bet the human asked for, in his words "start investing early even though
it's a risk" — the account exists so the verdict is a number against IWM and
against `drift`, and the sizing is a basket so no single knife decides it.
