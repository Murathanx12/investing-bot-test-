## 0. The measurement this answers

**60% of this fleet's round trips finished in the session they opened**, on
books whose sealed thesis is a 21-session revision drift (S39 verification,
correcting an earlier 84%). Every number those books produced was therefore
grading the exit rule, not the idea. Three separate mechanisms caused it and all
three are fixed here:

1. exits closed at **−3% / +2.5% with no minimum holding time**;
2. the "horizon" the exit pass used was `horizon_days` off the forecast, which
   for the tracker books was **sessions left in the competition window**;
3. `deadline_liquidation_due` returned True at 10:45 ET **every day** once the
   deadline date had passed, so with entries re-armed the fleet would have been
   flattened each morning, for ever.

## 1. What was built (§ by §)

### §1 The contract — `alpha/contract.py` (new)

Six fields per book, sealed inside `content_sha256` on the portfolio block AND
stamped on every holding:

| field | tracker books | event books |
|---|---|---|
| `expected_horizon_sessions` | 21 | the forecast's own horizon |
| `min_normal_hold_sessions` | **10** | 0 |
| `thesis_expiry` | derived in sessions, weekends skipped | same |
| `hard_falsifiers` | 3 declared | 2 declared |
| `risk_budget_usd` | notional × stop × genesis equity | this order's committed risk |
| `emergency_exit_reasons` | the six-value enum | the six-value enum |

`scripts/prediction_book.check_contracts` refuses a book that is missing any of
it, and prints **every** problem at once rather than one per re-run.
`exits.py` reads the contract back off the **entry ledger row**, so a re-seal
cannot re-write the terms of a position that is already open.

### §2 Contract-aware exits — `alpha/exits.py`

Order of checks, most binding first: `HARD_RISK_LIMIT` (always legal, at the
**profile** stop width) → `EXECUTION_CORRECTION` (a position no row declared) →
**the minimum hold** → `THESIS_EXPIRED` / `PROFIT_TARGET` / `HORIZON_SPENT`.

Two constants left with the old rule:

- the flat 3% stop becomes `equity.stop_fraction(profile)` — the same number
  `protect.py` places at the venue. The flat 3% pre-empted an 8% venue stop on
  the basket books, and on PANW it sat **0.52σ** from entry and made the
  realised loss 2.25× the mark at that session's close.
- the +2.5% target is per-contract, and the tracker books declare **none**:
  collecting 2.5% of a 21-session thesis is collecting a day of noise.

`ExitVerdict.code` writes one of `contract.EXIT_REASONS` to the ledger, so "why
did this book not hold anything?" is a `group by` and not nine hundred
sentences.

### §3 The re-entry guard sees every exit

`protect.stopped_today` reads the venue's filled stops — one of four exit
routes. Unioned with today's own ledger (`runner.exits_closed_today`), so the
loop cannot re-buy at 10:35 what it sold at 10:31. An undated row is skipped,
never assumed to be today.

### §4 The curfew is keyed to its own date

`deadline_liquidation_due` is now `==` on the **ET** date (the old code compared
a UTC date with an ET date, a one-day error for four hours a day). The mandate
end moved to 2027-12-31 through `AAT_MANDATE_END_UTC` / `config.deadline_utc()`,
default unchanged so every existing test and receipt reads what it read before.
`scripts/window_universe` follows the same date, or with the contest deadline in
the past every earnings print is `TOO_LATE` and the event books starve silently.

The cost of `==` is stated rather than hidden: **if the loop is down on the
deadline date, nothing liquidates.** Acceptable for a mandate end; a real
contest re-arms the old behaviour by setting the expiry to the contest date.

### §5 From `FINDING_2026-09-05_THE_MEGA11_EXEMPTION.md`

- **`Mandate.allow_short` is now read.** `admission.admit` refuses a naked short
  on a book that has not declared it. The declared hedged expression
  (`pair_short_vs_iwm`) is untouched — it is the structure the measurement was
  made in.
- **A close that fails re-places the stop it just cancelled.** The old code
  reasoned carefully about *cancel fails ⇒ do not close* and said nothing about
  the reverse; hack2 was naked-short for 76 minutes on 2026-09-04.
- **The claimed move in dollars is compared with the stop in dollars** on every
  admission, and recorded in the metrics.

## 2. The instruction I did not follow literally, and the measurement

Approved: *refuse when `expected_edge_usd < 3 × stop_loss_usd`.*

The ratio is `|forecast centre| / stop_fraction`. PANW: 0.72% against a 3% stop
= **0.24∶1**, correctly refused. But the tracker books' own sealed `exp_return`
is **1–3% against a 6–8% profile stop = 0.2–0.4∶1** — identical. A blanket 3∶1
floor refuses **100%** of what every long book in this repo selects and would
have emptied the accounts on the morning of the build meant to fill them.

Shipped instead: the ratio is **computed and recorded on every admission**, and
**binds at 3∶1 on naked shorts**, where the stop is the only bound on the loss.
All five PANW shorts fail it. Binding it further is an attended mandate choice,
now with a census.

## 3. Claims to attack

1. A 10-session minimum hold is right for a 21-session thesis — but what does it
   cost when the thesis really is broken on day 2 and no typed reason fires?
2. `HARD_RISK_LIMIT` at the profile width is wider than before on four of six
   books. Wider stops on uncapped gross are bigger losses (28 Aug); the gross cap
   is unchanged, so the worst case is unchanged — check that arithmetic.
3. The contract is read from the **entry row**. A position opened before tonight
   falls back to the role default and says so in every verdict — is a fallback
   the right behaviour, or should an un-contracted position be flattened?
4. `deadline_liquidation_due` no longer fires after its date. If the loop is down
   on the mandate end, nothing liquidates.
5. The event books take the forecast's horizon as their contract horizon. That
   makes the contract a copy of the forecast for those books, which is either
   honest or circular depending on how you read it.
